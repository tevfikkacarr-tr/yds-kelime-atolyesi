# Kelime tuzağı mikro testleri: @T çift (kelimeler / ile) / @s cümle (___) / @o seçenekler, doğru olan * ile / @e açıklama. Bloklar arası boş satır.

@T rise/raise
@s The company plans to ___ its prices next month.
@o rise|raise*
@e raise nesne alır (its prices): bir şeyi yükseltmek.

@T rise/raise
@s Prices ___ significantly last year because of the drought.
@o rose*|raised
@e rise nesnesizdir: fiyatlar kendiliğinden yükseldi. Geçmiş hali rose.

@T rise/raise
@s The report ___ serious concerns about the safety of the bridge.
@o rose|raised*
@e raise concerns = endişelere yol açmak, gündeme getirmek.

@T rise/raise
@s Sea levels are expected to ___ by at least 30 centimetres.
@o rise*|raise
@e Nesne yok: rise.

@T affect/effect
@s Air pollution can seriously ___ children's health.
@o affect*|effect
@e affect fiildir: etkilemek.

@T affect/effect
@s The new law had a positive ___ on employment.
@o affect|effect*
@e have an effect on: burada isim gerekiyor (a positive effect).

@T affect/effect
@s Scientists are studying the long-term ___ of microplastics.
@o affects|effects*
@e the effects of = -in etkileri (isim).

@T adapt/adopt
@s Many species have failed to ___ to the warmer climate.
@o adapt*|adopt
@e adapt to = -e uyum sağlamak.

@T adapt/adopt
@s Parliament voted to ___ the new policy on renewable energy without any amendments.
@o adapt|adopt*
@e adopt a policy = bir politikayı benimsemek; “without any amendments” değiştirmeyi (adapt) dışlar.

@T adapt/adopt
@s Students who move abroad often need time to ___ to a new culture.
@o adapt*|adopt
@e adapt to + isim.

@T economic/economical
@s The country experienced rapid ___ growth in the 1990s.
@o economic*|economical
@e economic = ekonomiyle ilgili.

@T economic/economical
@s Our grandmother was a very ___ cook who never wasted a single scrap of food.
@o economic|economical*
@e economical = tutumlu, savurgan olmayan; economic bir kişiyi nitelemez.

@T economic/economical
@s The country's ___ crisis deepened as unemployment and inflation continued to rise.
@o economic*|economical
@e economic crisis = ekonomik kriz (ekonomiyle ilgili); “economical” tutumlu demektir.

@T ensure/assure/insure
@s The doctor ___ the patient that the operation would not be painful.
@o ensured|assured*|insured
@e assure sb that = birine güvence vermek (kişi nesnesi alır).

@T ensure/assure/insure
@s Homeowners in the area should ___ their properties against flooding.
@o ensure|insure*
@e insure sth against sth = sigortalamak; ensure “against” ile bir mülkü nesne almaz.

@T respectively/respectfully
@s The two drugs reduced symptoms by 30 and 45 percent, ___.
@o respectively*|respectfully
@e respectively = sırasıyla.

@T respectively/respectfully
@s The students listened ___ while the guest speaker talked about her life.
@o respectively|respectfully*
@e respectfully = saygıyla.

@T considerable/considerate
@s The project required a ___ amount of time and money.
@o considerable*|considerate
@e considerable = hatırı sayılır, büyük.

@T considerable/considerate
@s It was very ___ of you to help your elderly neighbour.
@o considerable|considerate*
@e considerate = düşünceli, kibar.

@T comprehensive/comprehensible
@s The report is so ___ that it covers every level of the education system, from pre-school to university.
@o comprehensive*|comprehensible
@e comprehensive = kapsamlı; her düzeyi kapsamak anlaşılır olmanın (comprehensible) sonucu değildir.

@T comprehensive/comprehensible
@s Scientists should make their findings ___ to the general public.
@o comprehensive|comprehensible*
@e comprehensible to sb = biri için anlaşılır.

@T principal/principle
@s Rising sea levels are the ___ threat to coastal cities.
@o principal*|principle
@e principal = ana, başlıca (sıfat).

@T principal/principle
@s Equality is a basic ___ of democracy.
@o principal|principle*
@e principle = ilke (isim).

@T accept/except
@s She decided to ___ the job offer from the university.
@o accept*|except
@e accept = kabul etmek (fiil).

@T accept/except
@s The museum is open every day ___ Monday.
@o accept|except*
@e except = hariç (edat).

@T beside/besides
@s The new hospital was built ___ the river.
@o beside*|besides
@e beside = yanında (yer).

@T beside/besides
@s ___ being cheap, public transport is good for the environment.
@o Beside|Besides*
@e besides + V-ing = -in yanı sıra.

@T imply/infer
@s His silence seemed to ___ that he agreed with the decision.
@o imply*|infer
@e imply = ima etmek: mesajı veren taraf.

@T imply/infer
@s From the examples, readers can ___ the writer's opinion.
@o imply|infer*
@e infer = sonuç çıkarmak: mesajı alan taraf.

@T although/despite
@s ___ the heavy rain, the match went ahead as planned.
@o Although|Despite*
@e Ardından isim öbeği (the heavy rain) var: despite + isim.

@T although/despite
@s ___ it was raining heavily, the match went ahead as planned.
@o Although*|Despite
@e Ardından özne + yüklem (it was raining) var: although + cümle.

@T because/because of
@s The flight was cancelled ___ the storm.
@o because|because of*
@e Ardından isim: because of + isim.

@T because/because of
@s The flight was cancelled ___ the weather was too bad.
@o because*|because of
@e Ardından cümle: because + özne + yüklem.

@T lead to/result from
@s A diet high in sugar can ___ tooth decay, especially in young children.
@o lead to*|result from
@e Neden → sonuç: şekerli beslenme diş çürümesine yol açar (lead to); diş çürümesi şekerli beslenmenin nedeni olamaz.

@T lead to/result from
@s Most cases of tooth decay ___ a diet high in sugar.
@o lead to|result from*
@e Sonuç → neden: diş çürümesi şekerli beslenmeden kaynaklanır (result from); tersi anlamsızdır.
